Privacy notice

What we collect, and what we do with it.

This notice covers this website, our enquiries, and the automation tools we run for clients. It is written to be read, not filed. Last updated 2026-10-19.

Who we are

This website

Enquiries and visits

When you contact us by the form, by email or on WhatsApp, we receive what you send: typically your name, organisation, email address or phone number, and your message. We use it only to reply to you and, if you ask us to, to scope the work. It is kept for twelve months from the last message, then deleted, unless an engagement follows.

Visits are counted without cookies and without personal identifiers, so there is no cookie banner. We see which pages are read and in which language, not who read them.

We do not add you to a mailing list, share your details with anyone outside NEEL, or send you marketing because you once enquired.

The tools we run

If you are a customer of a business that uses one of our tools

The business you dealt with, our client, decides what is collected and why. We process that data on the client's behalf, under a written data processing agreement with every client, and only for the purpose the tool exists for: confirming a booking, replying to an enquiry, asking for a review, and so on.

Messages and calls from a tool say that they are automated. Every outbound message carries a way to stop, and a stop request is honoured within one working day. Marketing messages, such as review requests and win-back offers, go only to people who opted in.

Tool data is hosted in Malaysia or Singapore. Messages pass through the platforms involved, such as WhatsApp, under those platforms' own terms. Clinics and dental practices use our tools for booking and contact details only, never for symptoms or medical history.

When a client stops using a tool, their data is exported to them and our copy is deleted.

Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, withdraw consent you gave, and require us to stop sending you direct marketing. Write to info@neeladvisory.my; we reply within one working day and act within the time the Act allows. If the data belongs to a client's tool, we will put you in touch with that client or act on their instruction.

This notice may change as the tools change. The date at the top is the date of the current version.
