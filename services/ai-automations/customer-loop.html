Reviews, Asked Fairly

One request, same link for all.

Happy customers rarely leave a review unless someone asks at the right moment. After a paid bill, a closed booking or a completed order, every customer who opted in gets one short WhatsApp message with your review link, and one reminder a few days later. Then it stops.

No incentives, and no sorting of happy customers from unhappy ones first, as Google's rules require.

Customer Re-Engagement

Thirty, sixty, ninety days. Then it stops.

Lapsed customers rarely come back on their own. When a regular goes quiet, they get a personal note written around what they used to book, a different one at sixty days, and a last one at ninety. A reply or a booking ends the sequence.

30 60 90

Day 30

Hi Aina, it has been a month since your last visit. Your usual Saturday slot is open this week; reply here to book. Reply STOP to opt out.

Day 60

Hi Aina, just checking in. If anything has changed, tell us here, or book again in one tap. Reply STOP to opt out.

Day 90 · Last Note

Hi Aina, this is our last note for now. We would be glad to see you again whenever suits. Reply STOP to opt out.

Example messages. Yours are written for your business, with your sign-off.
