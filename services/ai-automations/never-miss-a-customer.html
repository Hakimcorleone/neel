01

Missed Call Text-Back

Built to order

Every missed call is a customer who may ring the next business on the list.

Example

A renovation contractor on site misses calls all day. Each unanswered call is forwarded to a short prompt: press 1 and we will WhatsApp you. Within a minute the caller has a message with the next step, after hours too.

Works with

  • Your existing mobile or landline, through call forwarding
  • WhatsApp Business
  • Your calendar or booking tool
  • Email as a fallback

Built for

  • Renovation contractors
  • Aircond and plumbing services
  • Car workshops
  • Property agents

Good to know

Callers who are not on WhatsApp get a short SMS without links, or nothing, as you prefer.

Runs on a dedicated business number we set up and run; your main number keeps working as it does today.

How it runs

  1. Call not answeredBusy and unanswered calls forward from your existing line to a Malaysian virtual number
  2. Caller opts inA five-second prompt: press 1 to get a WhatsApp message, so the first message is one they asked for
  3. Reply within a minuteAn approved WhatsApp template: what you do, that you are on a job, how to book or ask
  4. Conversation continuesReplies land in a shared WhatsApp inbox; urgent ones are flagged to you

02

AI Voice Receptionist

Built to order

Calls after six, over lunch and on weekends go to voicemail, and most callers do not leave one.

Example

A dental clinic closes at six and calls keep coming. The receptionist answers around the clock, says it is an automated assistant, takes the name, number, the service wanted and a preferred slot, then books it or passes it to the front desk in the morning.

Works with

  • Any Malaysian line, through call forwarding
  • Your calendar or booking tool
  • WhatsApp Business and email for summaries

Built for

  • Clinics and dental
  • Aesthetic clinics
  • Salons and spas
  • Aircond and plumbing services
  • Car workshops

Good to know

For clinics the receptionist takes booking and contact details only, never symptoms or medical history.

English now. A Bahasa Malaysia voice is offered once you have heard a recorded test call and approved it.

How it runs

  1. Call forwardedUnanswered or after-hours calls forward from your line
  2. Answered in EnglishOpens with “automated assistant, this call is recorded”, then a natural conversation
  3. Details taken, booking madeName, number, service and slot; booked in your calendar or held for the front desk
  4. You get the summaryA WhatsApp or email summary of every call, with the recording

03

Lead Follow-Up System

Built to order

An enquiry answered three hours later has usually gone to whoever answered first.

Example

A tuition centre gets twenty enquiries a week through its website form, Facebook and WhatsApp. Each one gets a reply within a minute, is asked two or three qualifying questions, and is offered a visit slot. The team sees every conversation in one inbox.

Works with

  • Lead forms and ad platforms
  • WhatsApp Business
  • Your calendar or booking tool
  • Your CRM, where you have one

Built for

  • Tuition centres
  • Clinics and dental
  • Property agents
  • Insurance and takaful agents
  • Agencies

Good to know

Follow-ups go only to people who enquired, and every message carries a way to stop.

For clinics: booking and contact details only.

How it runs

  1. Enquiry arrivesWebsite form, WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram lead form, Google Ads lead form, email
  2. Reply within a minuteA short, personal reply in the customer’s language, English or Bahasa Malaysia
  3. Qualified and routedTwo or three questions sort the enquiry by service and urgency; hot ones go straight to you
  4. Follow-up until answeredA timed sequence over WhatsApp and email that stops the moment the customer replies or books

Every tool

What every tool includes

  • A short call to confirm which systems you run and what the tool should do
  • Setup and connection to those systems, and a test week with your team
  • A dedicated WhatsApp Business number for the tool, set up and run by us
  • Messages written for your business, in English and Bahasa Malaysia, with your sign-off
  • Running it: hosting, monitoring, fixes, and normal usage within a fair-use cap
  • A weekly note of what the tool did, and a monthly number you can judge it by
  • A one-page data processing agreement, and export of your data if you leave

Questions

Asked before the first call

What does it cost?

A one-off setup fee and a monthly fee, quoted after a short call once we know which systems you run and how much traffic the tool will carry. The monthly fee covers running the tool, hosting, support and normal usage.

Whose accounts does it run on?

Ours. We set up and run the WhatsApp number, the AI and the hosting, so there is nothing for you to maintain or renew. If you would rather hold the accounts yourself, we hand them over.

What happens to our data if we stop?

We export it to you and delete our copy. Nothing is switched off without notice.

Can we start with one tool?

Yes, and most businesses should. Tools added later share the same number, the same data and the same weekly note.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the systems involved. Verifying a WhatsApp Business number takes a few days, connecting your booking or accounting tool a little longer, and we run a test week with your team before the tool goes live.

How do you handle personal data?

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 we act as your data processor. We sign a one-page data processing agreement, host in Malaysia or Singapore, message only people who opted in, put a way to stop in every message, and export your data to you if you leave. Our privacy notice has the detail.

Which tools suit a clinic?

Appointment reminders, the receptionist and enquiry follow-up, all limited to booking and contact details. We do not collect symptoms or medical history, and we do not put treatment offers in messages.
