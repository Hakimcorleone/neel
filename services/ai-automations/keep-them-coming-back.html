Review Request Automation

Built to order

Happy customers rarely leave a review unless someone asks at the right moment.

Example

A cafe serves two hundred customers a week and gets two reviews a month. Every customer who left a number at the counter or ordered online is asked for a Google review after the visit, with one reminder and no selecting.

Works with

  • POS and booking tools
  • Google Business Profile
  • WhatsApp Business
  • Email

Built for

  • Cafes and restaurants
  • Salons and spas
  • Car workshops
  • Gyms
  • Clinics and dental

Good to know

Every customer gets the same request. No incentives, and no sorting of happy customers from unhappy ones first, as Google’s rules require.

Only customers who opted in at the counter, in the booking form or on the receipt.

How it runs

  1. Visit completedA paid bill, a closed booking or an order marked done in your POS or booking tool

  3. One reminderA single follow-up a few days later if there is no reply, then it stops
  4. Reviews trackedA weekly note of review count and rating

03

Customer Re-Engagement

Built to order

Lapsed customers rarely come back on their own, and nobody has time to message them one by one.

Example

A gym has hundreds of members who stopped coming. Those who opted in get a personal message after thirty days, a different one at sixty, and a last one at ninety, each with a reason to come back and a way to stop.

Works with

  • Your membership, booking or POS records
  • WhatsApp Business
  • Email

Built for

  • Gyms
  • Salons and spas
  • Clinics and dental
  • Car workshops
  • Cafes and restaurants

Good to know

Only customers who gave marketing consent are messaged, from the next visit onwards. We never message an existing list that has not opted in.

No treatment offers in messages from clinics.

How it runs

  1. Inactivity detectedNo visit in thirty, sixty or ninety days, a membership near expiry, or a rule of your choosing
  2. Personal messageWritten around what they used to book or buy, in their language
  3. Sent with an opt-outAn approved WhatsApp template; every message carries a way to stop
  4. Return bookedA reply or a booking ends the sequence; results reported monthly

Questions

Asked before the first call

What does it cost?

A one-off setup fee and a monthly fee, quoted after a short call once we know which systems you run and how much traffic the tool will carry. The monthly fee covers running the tool, hosting, support and normal usage.

Whose accounts does it run on?

Ours. We set up and run the WhatsApp number, the AI and the hosting, so there is nothing for you to maintain or renew. If you would rather hold the accounts yourself, we hand them over.

What happens to our data if we stop?

We export it to you and delete our copy. Nothing is switched off without notice.

Can we start with one tool?

Yes, and most businesses should. Tools added later share the same number, the same data and the same weekly note.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the systems involved. Verifying a WhatsApp Business number takes a few days, connecting your booking or accounting tool a little longer, and we run a test week with your team before the tool goes live.

How do you handle personal data?

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 we act as your data processor. We sign a one-page data processing agreement, host in Malaysia or Singapore, message only people who opted in, put a way to stop in every message, and export your data to you if you leave. Our privacy notice has the detail.

Which tools suit a clinic?

Appointment reminders, the receptionist and enquiry follow-up, all limited to booking and contact details. We do not collect symptoms or medical history, and we do not put treatment offers in messages.
