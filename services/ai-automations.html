How Our Tools Behave

Three rules, printed once.

They apply to every tool, for every client, from the first message.

NEEL ADVISORY PLT · HOW OUR TOOLS BEHAVE

01. You are told when it is automated.

Every message and every call from a tool says that it is automated, and says which business it is from.

02. Every message carries a way to stop.

Only people who opted in are messaged, from their next visit onwards. We never message an existing list that has not said yes, and a stop request is honoured within one working day.

03. Your customers' data, held with care.

We act as your data processor under a one-page data processing agreement, host in Malaysia or Singapore, and export your data to you if you ever leave.

PRINTED ONCE

Channels

WhatsApp first, on a dedicated business number we set up and run, with approved message templates. Email second. SMS only for plain reminders without links.

Languages

English and Bahasa Malaysia for messages. The voice receptionist speaks English; Bahasa Malaysia is offered once you have heard a test call.

Accounts

The WhatsApp number, the AI and the hosting run on our accounts, so you have nothing to maintain. If you would rather hold them yourself, we hand them over.
