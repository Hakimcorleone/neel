What We Advise On

Eight areas, one team.

Most engagements touch several of these at once. A new outlet is a strategy question, a financial model, a marketing plan and an operations plan, and we treat it as one piece of work.

No. 01

Business strategy

Where the business should go, and what it should stop doing.

  • Market, customer and competitor review
  • Growth options weighed for risk, return and fit
  • Business plans and cases for owners, boards, banks and shareholders
  • The open decisions, listed, with our recommendation beside each
No. 02

Projections & financial modelling

The numbers behind the plan, in a model you can keep updating.

  • Revenue, cost, cash-flow and break-even projections
  • Scenarios and sensitivities before a commitment
  • Pricing and unit economics
  • Funding papers for banks, investors and grant bodies
No. 03

Marketing & growth

Positioning, channels and pricing that bring the right customers in.

  • Positioning and the offer
  • Channel and campaign plan, online and offline
  • Pricing and go-to-market
  • Measurement: what each ringgit of marketing returns
No. 04

Operations

How the work gets done every day, with fewer gaps.

  • Operating model and organisation
  • Processes, SOPs and controls
  • Capacity, service levels and outlet operations
  • Cost and margin improvement
No. 05

Procurement & vendors

Buying well, from tender to contract to supplier review.

  • Vendor selection and tendering
  • Contracts, service levels and pricing benchmarks
  • Make-or-buy and outsourcing decisions
  • Supplier performance reviews
No. 06

AI & automation advisory

Where AI and automation fit your business, and which process to automate first.

  • Process review: what to automate, what to leave alone
  • Tool and vendor selection, data readiness, governance

  • Built and run by us where that is quicker, through AI & Automations

Project & programme management

Owner-side delivery, so the plan moves without you doing it all.

  • Owner-side project management and a PMO where needed
  • Transformation and launch delivery

Performance management

Knowing, every month, whether the plan is working.

How an Engagement Runs

Four stages, in order.

Whatever the area, the work runs the same way. Start at the stage you need; each stage is built to hand over to the next.

01

Understand

A business diagnostic: market, customers, operations and the numbers, examined together.

You receive: a diagnostic report and a clear statement of the problem to solve

02

Decide

Options weighed for risk, return and fit; the strategy, the plan and the model.

You receive: a paper you can defend before a board, a bank or shareholders

03

Execute

A roadmap with owners, dates and milestones, owner-side project management, and the systems the plan depends on, built and run by us.

You receive: a plan in motion, with a weekly or fortnightly review

04

Sustain

KPIs, dashboards and monthly or quarterly reviews with management or the board, with an adviser on call between reviews.

You receive: a review cadence, and someone who already knows the plan
