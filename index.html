Services · End to end

Four stages, from diagnosis to review.

Engage us for one stage or for the whole sequence.

01Understand

Where the business stands

Market, customers, competitors and the numbers, examined together. Operations and processes as they run in practice. One clear statement of the problem to solve.

02Decide

The direction, and the case for it

Options weighed for risk and return, the chosen strategy, and the business plan, financial model and business case to take to owners, a board, a bank or shareholders.

03Execute

The roadmap, and the work on it

Owners, dates and milestones. Operating model, pricing and go-to-market. The systems the plan depends on, from customer channels to management reporting.

04Sustain

Keep it on track

KPIs and dashboards, monthly or quarterly reviews with management or the board, course corrections, and an adviser on call between reviews.
