Common questions

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Owner-run businesses in Malaysia, where the owner makes the decisions. Recent work includes training providers, service firms and small companies preparing for a bank or partner.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Yes. Each of the four services stands on its own. Many clients start with one and add another later.

Do you prepare business plans for bank loans?

Yes. We write the plan and a 12-month financial model you can edit. We are not licensed financial advisers, so we do not give investment or tax advice.

Who owns the website and documents after the project?

You do. The plan, the model, the site, the accounts and the documents stay in your name.
