Services

Business plans, financial models and strategy, in four stages.

Every engagement follows the same discipline, whether it is a single question or a multi-year programme. Start at the stage you need. Each stage is built to hand over to the next.

Stage 01 · Understand

Business diagnostic

When a decision is coming and the facts are spread across people, spreadsheets and opinion.

What we do

  • Market and competitor review
  • Customer, channel and pricing analysis
  • Unit economics and cost structure
  • Operations and process walk-through
  • Interviews with management, staff and, where useful, customers

What you receive

  • A diagnostic report with the numbers behind it
  • A clear statement of the problem to solve
  • The two or three questions the strategy must answer

Stage 02 · Decide

Business plan and financial model

When the options are known but the choice is not, or a board, bank, shareholder or parent company needs the case before it will approve.

What we do

  • Options weighed for risk, return and fit
  • The strategy itself, including what the business will stop doing
  • Business plan
  • Financial model covering revenue, costs, cash, break-even and scenarios
  • Business case or board paper

What you receive

  • A strategy paper the owner can defend before the board, the bank or the shareholders
  • A financial model you can update yourself
  • The open decisions, listed with our recommendation beside each

Stage 03 · Execute

Implementation, websites and automation

When the strategy exists but has not been put into motion, or doing so needs capacity the business does not have.

What we do

  • Roadmap with owners, dates and milestones
  • Operating model and organisation
  • Processes and controls
  • Pricing and go-to-market
  • Websites and customer channels
  • Automation of manual work, and management reporting
  • Built by us where that is quicker than buying them in
  • Programme management through to launch

What you receive

  • A plan in motion, with a weekly or fortnightly review cycle
  • One place where progress, owners and obstacles are visible
  • The systems themselves, in your name and under your control

Stage 04 · Sustain

KPI dashboards and performance reviews

When the plan is live and has to stay on course.

What we do

  • KPIs and dashboards
  • Monthly or quarterly performance reviews with management or the board
  • Course corrections as conditions change
  • An adviser on call between reviews

What you receive

  • A review cadence and the reports behind it
  • An adviser who already knows the plan when the next question arrives

Who we work with · Any size

From a single outlet to a GLC.

The questions change with size. The discipline does not.

  • Merchants and owner‑run businesses

    Opening a second outlet, restoring margins, deciding what to stop doing.

  • SMEs and family businesses

    Professionalising the business, succession, a new market or product line.

  • Corporations

    A division's strategy refresh, a business case for the board, a transformation that needs an owner.

  • GLCs and agencies

    Mandate reviews, performance frameworks, programmes that have to show results.

How we engage

Project, programme or ongoing advisory

A fixed-scope project answers a single question or covers one stage. A programme runs from diagnosis to a live plan, with one team throughout. Ongoing advisory keeps the plan under review after launch. Every engagement begins with a written scope, so both sides know what is being done and what it will produce. If you are unsure where to begin, start with the diagnostic: it produces that scope.

Scope

What we do not do

We are not lawyers, auditors, tax agents or licensed financial advisers. Where a matter needs a licence under Malaysian law, we say so and refer it to someone who holds one.
