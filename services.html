How we work

How we engage

Project, programme or ongoing advisory

A fixed-scope project answers a single question or covers one stage. A programme runs from diagnosis to a live plan, with one team throughout. Ongoing advisory keeps the plan under review after launch. Every engagement begins with a written scope, so both sides know what is being done and what it will produce. If you are unsure where to begin, start with the diagnostic: it produces that scope.

Anything we build along the way, from a website to a dashboard to one of the tools, is run by us for you on our accounts, so you have nothing to maintain. If you would rather hold it yourself, we hand it over.

What we do not do

We are not lawyers, auditors, tax agents or licensed financial advisers. Where a matter needs a licence under Malaysian law, we say so and refer it to someone who holds one.
