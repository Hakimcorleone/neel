Services

What we do, and
what you get.

Four services. What each one is for, and what you have at the end of it.

Strategy

Strategy and planning

For when you need to decide something with money attached, such as a new service, a new market, a partnership or the next twelve months.

What you get

  • A written recommendation covering the decision, the options considered, the numbers and the timeline
  • The assumptions we made, listed separately so you can challenge them
  • A plan of what happens next, in order, with who does what

Plans

Business plans and financial models

For when a partner, a bank, a landlord or a board wants to see the plan before saying yes.

What you get

  • A business plan with the market facts checked against primary sources
  • A twelve-month model of customers, revenue, costs, cash and break-even, built so you can change it yourself
  • The decisions still open, listed with our recommendation beside each one

Digital

Website, email and automation

For when the business needs a front door that works on a phone, an email address people trust, and the admin behind it to run itself.

What you get

  • A domain in your company's name, hosted and secured
  • Company email on your own domain, set up the way mail providers expect
  • A fast website that works on a phone, submitted to Google, and reviewed on a private link before it goes live
  • Brochure, poster and campaign email that match the site, so a date changed once is changed everywhere
  • An online form with the payment routes your customers use, and an order, invoice or confirmation issued automatically for each entry
  • A record of every entry, which the monthly report is built from

Ongoing

Ongoing management

For when it is built and you want it to keep working without hiring anyone to look after it.

What you get, every month

  • Domain, hosting and email kept running and secure
  • Dates, prices and listings kept current
  • Campaign material refreshed for each launch
  • A short report and a review call

Scope

What we do not do

We are not lawyers, auditors, tax agents or licensed financial advisers. Anything that needs a licence under Malaysian law, we refer to someone who holds one.

Which one is yours?

Your brief may need more than one of the four. Send what you have and we will scope it.
