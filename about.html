About

A business consultancy that stays for the execution.

NEEL Advisory is an independent business consultancy established in 2026. We work with organisations of every size on the decisions that shape them, and we stay to carry those decisions through: as consultants, as project managers, and as Solutions Partners inside the business.

Four Standards

What we hold to.

They apply to a two-week question and a two-year programme alike. How we work inside a business has its own page.

Written down

The scope, the assumptions and the open decisions are written down before work starts, and every recommendation is written down before it is acted on. Assumptions we have made are labelled as ours. Decisions that are yours are listed as yours.

Carried through

Delivering the document is the midpoint of an engagement. Our advice ends when the plan is live, and where the work needs capacity the business does not have, we provide it.

Built and run where it saves time

Where the strategy needs a system to carry it, such as a customer channel, a reporting line or an automated process, we build it and run it for you rather than wait for a vendor. If you would rather hold it yourself, we hand it over.

Confidential by default

We do not publish client names or engagements. What we learn inside a business stays inside it.
