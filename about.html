About

Small firm.
Finished work.

NEEL Advisory is a consulting partnership in Subang Jaya, Selangor, set up in 2026. We advise owner-run businesses and then build what we recommend, so every engagement ends in something you can open, send or run.

How an engagement runs

Brief to live, in four steps

The same path every time, whatever the size of the job.

  1. Brief

    You tell us what you sell, what is slow or manual, and what you want live.

  2. Plan

    A written recommendation, with the assumptions labelled and the decisions listed.

  3. Build

    The model, the site, the form or the automation, reviewed on a private link.

  4. Live

    Handed over working, and kept running if you want us to.

How we work

Three rules we keep

Written down

Every recommendation and assumption is in writing before work starts. If we assumed something, it is labelled as ours. If a decision is yours, it is listed as yours.

Advised, then built

We do not stop at the recommendation. The plan, the model, the site or the automation gets built and handed over working.

Yours to keep

The plan, the copy, the site and the documents we make for you are yours to keep.

Who it is for

Owner-run businesses

Businesses where the owner decides, and where the thinking and the building are better done by the same people. If you have a plan on paper and nobody to build it, or a business that runs on retyping, we can help.
