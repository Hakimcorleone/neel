How we work · The Solutions Partner

We come in, map it, build it, and keep it running.

One Solutions Partner, inside your business, from the first day to the day it runs without us. Four steps. Scroll to watch them play.

Watch how Get in touch

01 We come in

We start inside your business, not in a meeting room.

A Solutions Partner sits with the people who run the day, at the counter, in the inbox, in the spreadsheet that holds it all together. We watch what actually happens and write down what slips: the call missed after six, the booking typed twice, the quote nobody chased.

02 We map the systems

We map how everything connects, and agree what to build.

Every system you already run goes on one map: what feeds what, where things fall through. We put the options in front of you with a recommendation, and agree the scope in writing before anything is built.

03 We build and deploy

We build the automation and the AI agents, then switch them on.

Integrations, automations and agents, wired into your systems and tested on real work. Built and run on our accounts, so you have nothing to maintain. Deployed in stages, so the business keeps running while the new workflow goes live.

04 We keep it running

Then we keep it running.

The workflow runs on its own. We watch it, fix what breaks, and send a weekly note of what changed. Handed over, with code and access, if you ever prefer to run it yourself.

The difference

We do not just build you the tools. We run the workflow for you.

A tool needs someone to run it. A workflow run by us runs whether anyone remembers or not.

A tool, left with you

A workflow, run by us

The missed call is answered, the booking confirmed, the books updated, while your team serves customers. Nobody on your side has to open it, check it or remember it. That is the part we take on.
